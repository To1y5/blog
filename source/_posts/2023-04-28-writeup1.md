题目：

```php
<?php
highlight_file(__FILE__);
error_reporting(0);
$c=$_POST['c'];
if(!preg_match("/vi|less|tail|head|od|sh|echo|touch|re|mv|rm|cat|ls|tac|more|cut|curl|wget|base|>|<|`|\*|\\$|\\\/i",$c)){
    exec($c);
}else{
    die("hacker");
}
```
